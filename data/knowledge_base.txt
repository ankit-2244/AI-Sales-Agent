[COMPANY]
The Sales Agent is designed to turn a company website into a grounded sales representative on a live video call.

[PRODUCT]
The system is intended to help companies handle inbound interest and provide 24/7 sales conversations.

[LEAD_QUALIFICATION]
The agent should qualify leads, book demos, capture leads, and synchronize them to a CRM idempotently.

[KNOWLEDGE]
The system should ingest a company website, crawl and render pages, extract and classify information, and pull linked PDFs.

[GROUNDING]
The system should build a sourced company profile. Every fact should carry a source URL. Unsourced fields should be dropped and never inferred.

[RETRIEVAL]
The knowledge should be indexed for retrieval using semantic chunking, hybrid vector and BM25 search, and reranking.

[GUARDRAILS]
The system should have guardrails against invented pricing, discounts, commercial terms, delivery-date commitments, unsupported capability claims, unsupported compliance claims, and claims that cannot be traced to retrieved information.

[AI_DISCLOSURE]
The agent should disclose that it is an AI.

[EVALUATION]
The V1 target for groundedness is at least 95 percent. The V1 target for fabricated prices is zero.
